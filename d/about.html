About Archipixels

Two minds. A thousand ideas.

Every space has a story waiting to be told.

We're Shivika and Abhinav, partners in life, work and a shared curiosity for everything around us. Our love for design, travel, art and the unexpected has shaped the journey of Archipixels. We find inspiration in the places we visit, the people we meet and the smallest details that often go unnoticed.

We started Archipixels in 2018. Our journey began in Dubai, where we gained international experience before founding Archipixels in India. Now we're ready to take that energy worldwide. We're looking for collaborators who share our curiosity and clients with extraordinary ambitions.

How we think

  • Every space has a story

    We believe every space has a story waiting to be told. Design begins with curiosity about people, places and cultures.

  • Every design is new

    Every project begins with a different story, so every space gets an identity of its own.

  • Feeling comes first

    A space should feel a certain way and make you feel something, too. How it looks follows from that.
