Our approach

Spaces with stories of their own.

We believe every space has a story waiting to be told. At Archipixels, design begins with curiosity. A curiosity about people, places, cultures and the little things that often go unnoticed.

Spaces that are layered, expressive, sometimes unexpected, but always thoughtful in their details. We create artful, expressive spaces, each with its own identity. Every project begins with a different story, and we never repeat a design.

We don’t believe every space should look a certain way. We believe it should feel a certain way and make you feel something, too.

The founders

Two minds. A thousand ideas.

We’re Shivika and Abhinav, partners in life, work and a shared curiosity for everything around us. Our love for design, travel, art and the unexpected has shaped the journey of Archipixels.

We started Archipixels in 2018. Our journey began in Dubai, where we gained international experience before founding Archipixels in India. Now we’re ready to take that energy worldwide.
